PREAMBLE & ETHICAL PLEDGE

Commitment to Privacy

✓ In Plain English

We are a technology partner, not a data broker. We only gather information required to build, test, deploy, and scale your software and brand. We will never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or business data.

At M/s Anant Nova ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our"), represented by its founding partners Aaditya Chaudhary and Mohammad Nafis Ansari, we respect and protect the confidentiality of personal and business information entrusted to us by clients, users, and ecosystem collaborators.

This Privacy Policy sets forth our transparent data handling principles, explaining how we collect, use, process, secure, and retain your data when you interact with our website, platforms, software applications, or ecosystem delivery services.

SECTION 01

Statutory Compliance & Governing Framework

✓ In Plain English

This policy complies fully with Indian digital safety laws, including the landmark Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

This Privacy Policy is formulated, published, and enforced pursuant to:

  • Section 43A and Section 72A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 (as amended).
  • Rule 4 of the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
  • The provisions, accountability standards, and data fiduciary guidelines of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of the Republic of India.
SECTION 02

Categories of Information Collected

✓ In Plain English

We only ask for three types of information: (1) your contact details for legal contracts and invoicing; (2) server/API credentials to build your software; and (3) your internal catalogs or briefs to configure your product.

In our capacity as a Business & Technology Execution Ecosystem, we collect and process information strictly across three principal domains:

Category of Data Specific Data Elements Collected Primary Purpose of Collection
Client Identification & Contact Data Full legal name, official corporate entity name, designation, registered business address, business email address, direct contact numbers, and GST/PAN credentials. Contractual execution, client onboarding, project communication, and statutory tax invoicing.
Technical & System Infrastructure Data Staging server credentials, application API tokens, secure third-party secret keys, server environment configurations, database schemas, and DNS authentication records. Direct engineering, deployment, API integration, cloud hosting setup, and continuous software maintenance.
Proprietary Project Assets Internal product catalogs, operational workflows, business logic briefs, brand marketing assets, and seed customer lists supplied for system configuration. Custom software engineering, automation pipeline setup, and functional user acceptance testing.
SECTION 03

Server Access Protocols & Credential Integrity

✓ In Plain English

Both you and Anant Nova securely retain administrative keys during development. Our engineers never harvest, browse, or tamper with your live customer databases without your prior scheduled sign-off.

  • Dual-End Administrative Access: To ensure uninterrupted development, automated testing, and ongoing continuous deployment, server credentials and administrator logins shall remain securely held at both ends (Anant Nova and the designated Client representative).
  • Strict Prohibition on Arbitrary Data Access: Anant Nova explicitly covenants that its engineers, partners, and representatives shall never access, copy, harvest, download, or alter live production server data, customer databases, or transactional records unnecessarily. Access to live server environments will take place strictly during mutually agreed maintenance windows, scheduled code deployment runs, critical security patch updates, or upon the Client's explicit written direction.
SECTION 04

Data Retention Periods & Code Archival Protocols

✓ In Plain English

Temporary test files and customer sample lists are completely purged 30 days after project handover. We preserve encrypted backups of your custom source code indefinitely so you can always come back for future upgrades or disaster recovery.

  • Purging of Operational Client Records: Raw operational documents, temporary business records, test datasets, and customer lists supplied solely for system testing and integration will not be retained long-term. Such temporary data is securely expunged and permanently purged from active environments within thirty (30) calendar days following final project acceptance and handover.
  • Technical & Source Code Archival: Anant Nova securely archives custom source codes, repository versions, architectural schemas, and project configurations indefinitely within our encrypted private development infrastructure. This archival retention is strictly maintained to support the Client with emergency disaster recovery, bug isolation, security patch applications, and future feature upgrades.
SECTION 05

Third-Party Disclosures & Ecosystem Sharing

✓ In Plain English

We have a zero-tolerance policy against data sale. If an external specialist (like an industrial printer or SMS gateway) requires access, they receive only what is necessary, and only with your prior approval.

  • Zero Unauthorized Commercial Sale: Anant Nova maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding data monetization. We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal data, business proprietary ideas, or contact details to third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or commercial data brokers.
  • Need-to-Know Ecosystem Delivery: In delivering managed Tier 2 services (e.g., specialized print vendors, packaging manufacturers, bulk communication gateways), data disclosure is strictly restricted on a need-to-know basis and executed only upon prior Client approval.
  • Statutory & Legal Compulsion: We may disclose data without prior notice only if compelled by a valid subpoena, court order, or official directive from an authorized statutory law enforcement authority under Indian law.
SECTION 06

Client Portfolio Policy & The Privacy Note (NDA)

✓ In Plain English

By default, we may showcase finished web designs and case studies in our public portfolio. If you are building in stealth mode or require 100% white-label confidentiality, simply request an NDA before we start, and we will never publish your project publicly.

To demonstrate our technical capabilities to prospective clients, Anant Nova reserves the right to present completed websites, visual interfaces, public web URLs, and case study overviews across our digital portfolio and promotional channels.

Stealth & White-Label Protection (Privacy Note / NDA)

If a Client requires strict confidentiality, stealth startup status, or complete white-label non-disclosure, the Client must formally request and execute a specific Privacy Note / Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) prior to project initiation. Upon approval, Anant Nova guarantees that the client's brand, software, and deliverables will remain strictly excluded from all public portfolio displays and case studies.

SECTION 07

Data Security Measures & Breach Management

✓ In Plain English

We safeguard your repositories and systems using SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption in transit, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and isolated private development environments.

Anant Nova implements industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, including:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption for all data in transit across web portals and APIs.
  • Encrypted Development Environments with localized environment variables and secret managers.
  • Access Controls with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enforced across internal infrastructure and code repositories.
  • Segmented Repositories & Zero-Trust Boundaries preventing cross-project code contamination.

While no digital infrastructure is entirely invulnerable, we conduct continuous monitoring to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure, and maintain an active incident response procedure.

SECTION 08

Client Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

✓ In Plain English

Under Indian data protection law, you have full control. You can request a summary of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to fix errors, or request erasure once our contract is complete.

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Clients and data principals retain the right to:

  1. Request Access: Obtain a transparent summary of personal data processed by us and the processing activities undertaken.
  2. Request Correction & Updating: Direct the correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate or misleading personal data.
  3. Request Erasure: Request the deletion and erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the contractual purpose or statutory compliance.
SECTION 09

Jurisdiction & Dispute Resolution

✓ In Plain English

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. In the event of a dispute, it will be resolved through arbitration seated in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and interpreted under the substantive laws of the Republic of India.

Any disputes arising out of data privacy practices, processing, or confidentiality breaches shall be resolved via arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. The competent courts in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, shall hold exclusive territorial jurisdiction.
