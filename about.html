GOVERNANCE & STANDARDS

The Delivery Model & Quality Standard

We operate three transparent delivery pathways, each held to a strict 8-factor vetting standard to ensure client protection.

CORE DELIVERY

Executed directly by Anant Nova core personnel. Daily standups, transparent GitHub/GitLab repositories, unified design systems, and direct sprint Slack/Discord communication.

PARTNER DELIVERY

The 8-Factor Partner Vetting Protocol

Before any studio or provider enters Layer 02 or 03, they must pass rigorous evaluation across eight critical criteria:

01
Technical Proficiency: Demonstrated architectural mastery and clean, documented output.
02
Verified Track Record: Validated portfolio of successful, high-retention live deployments.
03
SLA & Turnaround Speed: Consistent delivery against agreed sprint milestones.
04
Clear Communication: Proactive milestone reporting with zero jargon confusion.
05
Security & Compliance: Strict NDA adherence, IP ownership assignment, and data privacy.
06
Fair & Transparent Cost: Honest pricing structures without hidden markups or sudden fees.
07
Ethical Integrity: Commitment to quality craftsmanship and transparent business conduct.
08
Cultural & Vision Alignment: Obsession with client success and high standards of excellence.
