SECTION 04

Cancellation, 30% Completion Threshold & Damages

✓ In Plain English

If you cancel early before 30% progress, you pay only for hours worked (quantum meruit). Once 30%+ of the project is completed (designs approved, core code written, or elapsed milestones), the advance is strictly non-refundable liquidated damages because senior engineering teams were reserved for you.

Voluntary Cancellation by Client: The Client may request project termination via official written communication. However, the Client remains legally liable to compensate Anant Nova on a quantum meruit basis for all work executed, engineering hours expended, and non-cancellable partner commitments incurred up to the date of notice receipt.

PHASE 1: 0% TO 29% PROGRESS

Quantum Meruit Reconciliation

Client compensates strictly for hours expended and non-cancellable vendor commitments. Any surplus unexpended advance balance is liquidated.

PHASE 2: ≥ 30% PROGRESS

30% Threshold Forfeiture Protocol

Entire advance deposit & milestone sums stand strictly non-refundable and forfeited as liquidated damages. Zero source code access granted.

The 30% Completion Threshold Forfeiture: Where a project has achieved 30% (thirty percent) progress or higher—as determined by design approvals, sprint deployments, core architecture setup, backend APIs, or elapsed project timeline:

  1. The entire advance deposit and any subsequent milestone sums paid to date shall stand strictly non-refundable and forfeited as pre-estimated liquidated damages for dedicated team allocation and technical setup.
  2. The Client shall possess no legal title, license, or access to raw source code repositories, staging web links, proprietary scripts, server databases, wireframes, or administrative panels.
  3. The Client may elect to place the project on a formal written hold (not exceeding 60 calendar days) or proceed with complete termination under the forfeiture terms above.

Prohibition of Unlawful Escalation

Any bad-faith escalation, groundless chargeback attempt, public slander, or defamatory conduct by the Client against Anant Nova or its partners shall constitute a material breach of contract. Anant Nova reserves the statutory right to institute immediate legal proceedings under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, and the Commercial Courts Act, 2015, for the recovery of unpaid dues, contractual penalties, and exemplary reputational damages.
